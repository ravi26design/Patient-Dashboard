# Parent Engagement — Why a Parent Uses the App

> Product/strategy note. Goal: **a connected parent opens the app at least once every day.**

Daily opens come from one thing that (a) answers the parent's biggest daily emotion and
(b) can't be gotten anywhere else. For a parent of someone in OUD recovery that emotion is
**worry**. The daily hook has to convert **worry → reassurance in ~5 seconds.**

## Why a parent uses the app

1. **Peace of mind** — "Is my kid okay *today*?" (the #1 daily driver)
2. **Being useful** — a way to *help* instead of nagging
3. **Staying connected** — without being intrusive
4. **Hope** — seeing progress add up (streaks, healing, milestones)

## The daily hook — a Parent "Daily" screen

One screen, three stacked hooks, top to bottom:

### 1. On-track status + streak — the reassurance glance
- ✅ **"Alex is on track today"** — checked in / took medication (a simple green/amber
  state, **never** clinical detail)
- 🔥 **Day 47** — the streak ticks up daily
- 📈 Recovery Health trend arrow — **Stable ↑**

The streak number changing every day is why the card is never "the same as yesterday."

### 2. Reward countdown — skin in the game
- 🎁 **"3 days until 'Dinner at Olive Garden'"** — *the reward you pledged at 50 days*

Ties the parent's own promise to a ticking date; they check to watch it get closer.

### 3. Send encouragement — gives them a job
- 💬 One tap → a short note the patient sees in-app ("Proud of you 💚")
- Prompted once daily; pre-written options + free text

The secret weapon: it turns a passive, worried parent into an **active supporter**, and it
**helps the patient too** — the same daily open benefits both sides (a reciprocal loop that
compounds retention).

## The trigger that makes it daily

A single gentle morning push notification:

> 🌱 *"A quiet update on Alex is ready."* — calm, never alarming.

If the streak is at risk or a reward is one day away, the nudge gets slightly more specific
("Alex is 1 day from your reward 🎁").

## Why it works (the psychology)

| Mechanic | Why it drives daily opens |
|---|---|
| **Daily status + streak** | Worry resets every 24h; the streak number is a reason to check |
| **Push nudge** | Gentle, not alarming — "a quiet update is ready" |
| **Reward countdown** | Skin in the game — the parent pledged it |
| **Send-support prompt** | Gives the parent a job; also helps the patient (reciprocal) |
| **Recovery education** | "Day 47: endorphins are restarting" — learn something new daily |

- **Worry resets every 24h** → a daily reason to look
- **The numbers move daily** (streak, countdown) → never stale
- **Reciprocity** → sending support feels good *and* helps their kid
- **Loss aversion** → "don't break the streak / the reward is so close"

## Pieces we already have to assemble this

## Open questions to decide before building

- **Privacy line** — how much does the *patient* control what a parent sees?
  Recommended default: **patient opt-in per item** (status ✓, streak ✓, health trend
  optional). Trust is what keeps the whole family feature alive long-term.
- **Parent access** — is this a separate parent login / companion view, or a section the
  patient shows family? This decides the auth model.
